# Search Strategy

## Overview

This document details the search strategy employed in the systematic literature review on cybercrime glorification and youth radicalisation.

## Search Queries

Three complementary search strings were designed to capture the phenomenon from different angles:

### S1 — Cybercrime Glorification and Notoriety

**Focus:** Direct references to the glorification, celebration, or prestige associated with cybercrime.

**Terms:** `cybercrime` OR `cybercriminal` OR `hacker` combined with `glorification` OR `notoriety` OR `prestige` OR `celebration` OR `flex` OR `media portrayal`

### S2 — Youth Pathways into Cybercrime

**Focus:** How young people are drawn into cybercriminal activity, including motivational and social factors.

**Terms:** `youth` OR `juvenile` OR `teenager` OR `adolescent` combined with `cybercrime` OR `hacking` OR `cyber offending` combined with `pathway` OR `motivation` OR `recruitment` OR `radicalisation` OR `social learning`

### S3 — Hacker Culture and Subculture

**Focus:** The cultural dimensions of hacking communities, including identity, status, and reputation systems.

**Terms:** `hacker culture` OR `hacker subculture` OR `hacker identity` OR `hacker community` combined with `prestige` OR `reputation` OR `status` OR `identity` OR `ethics`

## Time Period

No date restrictions were applied during the initial search. The final included studies span **1990–2026**.

## Search Execution Dates

Searches were conducted between **March–April 2026**.

## Deduplication

Title-based matching across databases identified and removed **64 duplicate records**, reducing the pool from 917 to **853 unique records**.
